🔒 Privacy Policy

RAAZ4 - Dark Psychology App

1. Introduction

Welcome to RAAZ4. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Dark Psychology educational app.

RAAZ4 is committed to protecting your privacy. We store everything locally on your device only.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name (display name you choose)
  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Password (stored locally only)
  • Quiz scores and completed topics
  • Selected language preference

We do NOT collect: email, phone number, location, or any personal identification.

3. How We Store Your Data

All data is stored exclusively in your device's browser LocalStorage.

100% Local Storage - Your data stays on YOUR device only. We cannot access it.

4. Third Party Services

RAAZ4 does NOT use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Voice feature uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech only. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

5. Age Restriction

⚠️ This app is intended for users aged 18 and above. Content is for educational purposes only.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete all data by clearing browser storage
  • Access your data (visible in the app)
  • Stop using the app at any time
