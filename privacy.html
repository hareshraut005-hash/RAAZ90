🔒 Privacy Policy

RAAZ4 - Dark Psychology App

1. Introduction

Welcome to RAAZ4 ("App", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Dark Psychology educational app.

RAAZ4 is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect minimal data and store everything locally on your device only.

2. Information We Collect

RAAZ4 collects only the following information that you provide:

  • Name (display name you choose)
  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Password (stored locally only)
  • Quiz scores and completed topics
  • Selected language preference

We do NOT collect: email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or any personal identification information.

3. How We Store Your Data

All data is stored exclusively in your device's browser LocalStorage. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • We have no servers storing your information
  • No cloud backup of personal data
  • Data is deleted when you clear browser data
  • Only you can access your data on your device
100% Local Storage - Your data stays on YOUR device only. We cannot access it.

4. Third Party Services

RAAZ4 does NOT use any third party services including:

  • No Google Analytics or tracking
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integration
  • No external APIs for user data
  • No cloud databases

Voice feature uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech system only. No audio data is recorded or transmitted.

5. Content and Age Restriction

RAAZ4 contains educational content about Dark Psychology which includes topics related to manipulation, psychological abuse, and dark personality traits.

⚠️ This app is intended for users aged 18 and above. The content is for educational and awareness purposes only.

By using this app, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older.

6. Educational Purpose

All content in RAAZ4 is strictly for educational and awareness purposes. The information about dark psychology, manipulation tactics, and related topics is provided to:

  • Help users recognize manipulative behavior
  • Provide knowledge for self-protection
  • Increase psychological awareness
  • Support academic understanding of psychology

This content should NOT be used to harm, manipulate, or exploit others.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device security. We recommend:

  • Using a strong device PIN or password
  • Not sharing your device with others
  • Logging out when done if using shared device

8. Children's Privacy

RAAZ4 is NOT intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used this app, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the new policy.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all your data by clearing browser/app storage
  • Access your data (it is all visible in the app)
  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns
